Reminder: this chart does NOT run database migrations. Before the first deploy to a new
environment, run job-service/scripts/migrate.ts (and validate-schema.ts) against the
target database out-of-band, then scripts/backfill-job-service.ts from the monolith
- see job-service/README.md and MIGRATION_RUNBOOK.md.

This service has no gateway route yet. It receives a live dual-written copy of every jobs row on
create/update/delete (see src/dualWrite.ts's upsertJob/patchJob/deleteJobMirror, gated behind
DUAL_WRITE_ENABLED, off by default). Its own GET /internal/jobs and /internal/jobs/:id are real and
ready for a future caller once cut over.
